## Certification & Licensing Pipeline

### Certification Mark Status: UK00004343685

- **Status:** In examination (UK IPO, Examiner: Lisa Skeggs)
- **Classes:** 41 (Education services), 42 (Professional services), 45 (Legal services)
- **Governance:** The Burgess Principle Limited (Co. No. 17199287)
- **Expected grant:** Q3 2026 (provisional)
- **Certified use:** Use of the mark signals real individual human scrutiny, not a badge on an unchanged system

### Tier 1 Certification (Premium)

Eligibility: Organisations with documented full implementation (Tier 1 adoption above).

**Criteria:**
- SOVEREIGN classification ≥ 95% of significant decisions affecting individuals
- Individual human review documented for each SOVEREIGN decision
- NULL/AMBIGUOUS escalation protocol active and auditable
- Independent verification (annual audit)

**Benefit:** Right to display UK00004343685 certification mark in marketing and compliance documentation.

**Cost:** £5,000 annual certification fee (provisional); covers audit + mark license.

### Tier 2 Governance License (Standard)

Eligibility: Organisations in governance adoption phase (Tier 2 above) or planning implementations.

**Criteria:**
- Committed to SOVEREIGN/NULL/AMBIGUOUS classification in governance scope
- Governance documentation filed with The Burgess Principle Limited
- Commitment to independent verification within 12 months

**Benefit:** License to reference "adopting Burgess Principle governance" in internal and external communications (not the certification mark).

**Cost:** £1,500 annual governance license.

### Tier 3 Community License (Free)

Eligibility: Any organisation using the framework for internal governance or research.

**Criteria:**
- Attribution to github.com/ljbudgie/burgess-principle
- Public commitment to transparency (operations or findings)

**Benefit:** Free use; community recognition on this tracker.

**Cost:** None (MIT-licensed framework).

## How to Apply for Adoption Recognition

### To register a Verified Adoption (Tier 1 or 2):

1. **Complete the adoption questionnaire:**
   ```
   Organisation name:
   Sector:
   Implementation scope: (specific decisions/systems)
   Status: (live / in pilot / planned)
   Lead contact: (name, role, email)
   Documentation: (internal policy, case study, audit result)
   Willingness to contribute to case study: (yes/no)
   ```

2. **Submit via:**
   - GitHub issue: [Open an issue](https://github.com/ljbudgie/burgess-principle/issues/new)
   - Email: adoption@burgess-principle.limited (when in-service)
   - Or create a pull request updating this file

3. **Review process:**
   - Verification of adoption claims (public records, regulatory citations, case studies)
   - Publication on this tracker with your approval
   - Invitation to contribute public case study or testimonial

### To apply for Certification or Governance License:

1. **Contact:** The Burgess Principle Limited
2. **Provide:** Evidence of adoption (documentation, audit trail, SOVEREIGN %)
3. **Undergo:** Independent verification audit (Tier 1) or governance assessment (Tier 2)
4. **License:** Mark or governance license issued; use terms supplied

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can we use the Burgess Principle without adopting it formally?

**A:** Yes. The framework is MIT-licensed. Use it in your governance, audit, or operations without formal adoption. Attribution to the repository is appreciated but not required.

### Q: What's the difference between Tier 1 and Tier 2?

**A:** Tier 1 = fully operational (SOVEREIGN/NULL/AMBIGUOUS classification in live systems). Tier 2 = committed to adoption but still implementing. Both are "verified" adoptions.

### Q: Can we get certified right now?

**A:** Not yet. Certification mark (UK00004343685) is in examination. Provisional mark licensing expected Q3 2026. Governance licenses (Tier 2) are available now.

### Q: How do we know if an organization is really adopting, not just claiming?

**A:** This tracker only includes verified adoptions with public evidence (PR merge, case study, regulatory mention, audit result). We encourage independent verification.
