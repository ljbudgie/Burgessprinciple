# Mortgage Decision / Underwriting – Request for Human Review  
**— The Burgess Principle**

Use this when a mortgage lender, broker, or packager has refused, delayed,
restricted, or varied a mortgage application, product transfer, remortgage,
forbearance request, or related underwriting decision, and it is unclear
whether a named human reviewed *your* specific facts before acting.

This is not regulated mortgage advice. It is a calm request for confirmation
of individual human scrutiny. If you need debt or housing advice, contact a
qualified adviser (for example MoneyHelper or a housing charity).

---

**Date:** [DATE]  
**Reference:** [Application / Account / Case Reference]  
**To:** [Lender / Broker / Underwriting / Complaints Team]

Dear [Team],

I hope this finds you well.

I am writing about [briefly describe the decision in one sentence, e.g. "the
refusal of my residential mortgage application dated [date]", "the decline of
my product transfer", "the delay / additional conditions on my remortgage", or
"the handling of my forbearance / payment-difficulty request"].

Following the guidance in the Burgess Principle, I would like to kindly ask:

**Was a human member of your team able to personally review the specific facts of *my* individual situation before this mortgage decision was made?**

If someone did review my case, I would be grateful for a short written
confirmation including:

1. The name and role of the reviewer (or an internal reference that identifies them).  
2. The date of that review.  
3. Which of my specific facts were considered (for example income type and
   variability, expenditure, deposit source, credit history explanations,
   dependants, disability or vulnerability markers, or property circumstances).  
4. Confirmation that the review took place **before** the decision was issued
   to me.

If that did not happen, I would appreciate my application now receiving that
personal human attention, rather than remaining the product of automated
affordability scoring, credit rules, fraud flags, or standard policy triggers
alone.

For clarity, a general statement that your systems include human oversight,
that decisions may be reviewed, that the matter was handled in line with FCA
rules or Consumer Duty, or that "edge cases" are escalated, does **not**
confirm that a human member of your team personally reviewed the specific
facts of my case. Please provide a direct **YES** or **NO** answer to the
Burgess Principle question above. If **YES**, please also identify the
reviewer by name and role.

So that I can understand and respond, please also confirm:

1. What exact decision was made and when it first took effect.  
2. Whether the decision relied on automated underwriting, credit scoring,
   open-banking affordability tools, third-party data, or fraud / AML flags.  
3. Whether my application was treated as a standard automated path or
   escalated to manual underwriting, and why.  
4. What further information, if any, you need from me for a fair individual
   review.  
5. How I can escalate under your complaints process (FCA DISP) if needed,
   including the date from which any final-response clock runs.

Optional facts you may wish me to clarify (I can supply these on request):

- **Income type:** [employed / self-employed / mixed / other]  
- **Circumstances the form may not have captured:** [e.g. recent childcare
  costs, disability-related expenditure, temporary income dip, gift deposit
  evidence, shared ownership specifics]  
- **Vulnerability / accessibility needs:** [if any]

I am keen to cooperate. I simply ask that my situation be treated as an
individual mortgage case requiring real human judgment.

Thank you for your time.

I am also requesting reasonable adjustments under the Equality Act 2010
(sections 20 and 29) if applicable. My preferred method of communication is
email only.

Yours sincerely,  
[Your Full Name]  
[Your Contact Details]  
[Application / Account reference]
