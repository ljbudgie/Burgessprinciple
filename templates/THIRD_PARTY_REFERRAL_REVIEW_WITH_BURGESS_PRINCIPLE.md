# Third-Party Referral / Assessment Review
**— The Burgess Principle**

**Use this when** an institution has based, or intends to base, a decision about
you on an assessment carried out by a **third party** — for example a medical
examiner, occupational health provider, independent expert assessor, actuarial
model, or contracted assessment service (e.g. a work capability assessment, an
insurance medical, an OH report, a fitness-to-work or fitness-to-practise
opinion, or an expert report relied on in a dispute).

A referral to a third party does **not** by itself satisfy meaningful human
involvement. The Burgess question applies at **two points**:

1. **The assessment itself** — did a named individual at the third party
   personally review *your* specific facts, or were you processed against a
   template, a checklist, or a model?
2. **The reliance decision** — did a named human at the referring institution
   personally consider *your* specific facts before adopting the third party's
   conclusion, or was the outcome simply transferred from the report into the
   decision?

**Date:** [Date]
**Reference:** [Your Case / Claim / Decision Reference]
**To:** [Referring Institution and/or Third-Party Assessor — Name & Full Address]

---

Dear [Institution / Assessment Team],

I am writing about [briefly describe the decision and the referral in one
sentence, e.g. "the work capability decision dated 14 May 2026, which relied on
an assessment carried out by [assessor]"].

Following the Burgess Principle, I would like to ask one question of both the
assessment and the decision that relied on it:

**Was a named human member of the team able to personally review the specific
facts of *my* situation — first when the assessment was carried out, and again
before its conclusions were adopted into the decision affecting me?**

To help me understand how my case was handled, please confirm in writing:

1. The **name and professional role** of the individual who personally carried
   out or reviewed my assessment.
2. The **specific facts about me** that they reviewed (my records, history, and
   individual circumstances — not population averages, templates, or a
   standardised script).
3. Whether the assessment was generated, scored, or shortlisted by any
   **automated tool or model**, and if so, what human review was applied to its
   output before it was relied upon.
4. The **name and role** of the person at [referring institution] who
   personally considered my specific facts before adopting the assessment's
   conclusion, and confirmation that they had authority to depart from it.

For clarity, a general statement that the assessment was "carried out by a
qualified professional," that it followed "standard procedure," or that the
decision was "subject to human oversight" does not confirm that a named human
personally reviewed the specific facts of my case at either point. Please
provide a direct **YES** or **NO** to the question above. If **YES**, please
identify the reviewer(s) by name and role and describe what they reviewed.

If that individual review did not take place at either point, I would be
grateful for it to happen now, before any further action is taken, and to be
told the result.

I am happy to provide any further information that would help.

Thank you for treating this as the individual matter it is. I look forward to
your reply by [suggest a reasonable date, e.g. 14 days from now].

I am also requesting reasonable adjustments under the Equality Act 2010
(sections 20 and 29) if applicable. My preferred method of communication is
email only.

Yours sincerely,
[Your Full Name]
[Your Contact Details]
