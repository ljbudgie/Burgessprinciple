## 1. Government Precedent Statement — universal opener

Use at the start of any formal letter or submission. Keep it intact; do not paraphrase the Hansard citation.

---

> On 5 March 2026, the Home Secretary announced the conversion of asylum support under the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 from a blanket statutory duty into a conditional, evidence-based power — requiring a named caseworker to review the specific facts of each individual case before the power to withhold or withdraw support is exercised. HC Deb 5 March 2026 (Migration Reform statement, Shabana Mahmood).
>
> This is not a campaigning argument. It is the Government's enacted position on when blanket automated processing is and is not acceptable: where individual circumstances are relevant to entitlement or the exercise of power, a named human must assess those circumstances individually before the power is exercised — and the decision record must be attributable and open to judicial review.
>
> I am asking you to apply the equivalent standard to my case.

## 2. Energy companies — unsigned warrants and automated billing decisions

**Use for:** E.ON Next, British Gas, any energy supplier using automated warrant applications, bulk billing decisions, or debt recovery without individual review.  
**Anchor:** E.ON unsigned warrant EG021819-26; Rights of Entry (Gas and Electricity Boards) Act 1954.

---

> The Home Office, administering one of the most operationally complex support systems in government, has enacted that conditional powers affecting individuals must be preceded by a named caseworker's individual review of specific facts. Forced entry to a domestic property — one of the most serious exercises of institutional power available to an energy supplier — requires at minimum the equivalent standard.
>
> Please identify, by name and role, the person who individually reviewed the specific facts of my case — including [the warrant application dated [DATE] / the debt recovery instruction / the billing decision of [DATE]] — before institutional power was exercised against me.
>
> A system log, a team reference, or a general statement of human oversight processes does not satisfy this request. I am asking for the named individual who applied their professional judgment to my specific facts before [the warrant was sought / the enforcement was instructed / the charge was applied].
>
> Please also confirm whether the warrant was signed and dated by a named justice of the peace before execution, as required under the Rights of Entry (Gas and Electricity Boards) Act 1954 s.2. If no named individual can be identified for the internal decision or the judicial authorisation, the decision is **NULL** and must be treated as the documented starting point for repair.

## 3. Local authorities — PCN, council tax, and enforcement agent instruction

**Use for:** Darlington Borough Council, any local authority automating PCN issuance, council tax liability orders, or enforcement agent instruction without individual review.  
**Anchor:** TPT DK00003-2605 (allowed 14 June 2026); LGO 26000967; Equality Act 2010 s.149 (PSED).

---

> The Traffic Penalty Tribunal (DK00003-2605, decision 14 June 2026) found that Darlington Borough Council had not established the fundamental legal prerequisite — keepership of the vehicle — before pursuing enforcement, because no named human had individually reviewed the specific facts of the specific case. The appeal was allowed and the enforcement action overturned. This is the first external judicial validation of the Burgess Principle binary test in a live enforcement case.
>
> The Home Office has separately enacted in statute that conditional powers affecting individuals require a named caseworker's review of specific facts before the power is exercised. The same standard applies to parking enforcement, council tax liability, and enforcement agent instruction.
>
> Please confirm: which named officer reviewed the specific facts of [PCN [reference] / the liability order application / the enforcement agent instruction dated [DATE]] before that power was exercised against me? If no named officer individually reviewed those specific facts, the decision is **NULL**.
>
> I ask you to [withdraw the enforcement action / treat this as a formal complaint under your complaints procedure / provide the information required under a Subject Access Request]. I am also asserting my rights under the Public Sector Equality Duty (Equality Act 2010 s.149) and request that my reasonable adjustment of email-only communication be recorded on my file.

## 4. Medical devices and NHS audiology — algorithmic clinical settings

**Use for:** Phonak AutoSense OS, NHS Foundation Trust audiology, any clinical setting in which algorithmic device programming replaces individual clinical review.  
**Anchor:** MHRA FOI2026/00527; CEC 253215; Medical Devices Regulations 2002; Equality Act 2010 s.20.

---

> Where individual circumstances are relevant to treatment, Parliament requires a named human to assess those circumstances individually before institutional power is exercised. The Home Office conditionality reform illustrates this at the highest level of government administration. The same principle applies to clinical settings.
>
> A clinical device fitted or programmed for a specific patient without a named clinician reviewing that patient's specific audiogram, specific hearing profile, and specific expressed preferences is processing the patient as a member of a class — not as an individual. That is structurally **NULL** under the Burgess binary test, regardless of the sophistication of the algorithm applied.
>
> Please confirm: which named clinician reviewed the specific clinical facts of my case — including [my audiogram dated [DATE] / my asymmetric hearing profile / my stated preference for [adjustment]] — before the clinical decision to [program the AutoSense OS settings / select this fitting protocol / discharge me from the pathway] was made and implemented?
>
> If no named clinician individually reviewed those specific facts, I ask that the decision be reviewed by a named audiologist with authority to change the outcome, and that the review be documented in a tamper-evident clinical record. Please also confirm compliance with the Medical Devices Regulations 2002 (clinical oversight obligations for Class IIa algorithmic devices) and Equality Act 2010 s.20 (anticipatory reasonable adjustment duty for disabled patients).

## 5. Banks and financial services — automated credit and account decisions

**Use for:** Vanquis, Equifax, Trading 212, any bank or financial institution relying on automated credit scoring, account restriction, or fraud classification without individual review.  
**Anchor:** UK GDPR Articles 22A–22D (DUAA 2025); FCA Consumer Duty (July 2023).

---

> The Home Office has confirmed in enacted law that blanket processing of individuals by reference to class or status — without a named human reviewing individual facts — is insufficient where individual circumstances are relevant to entitlement. This applies with equal force to automated credit decisions, account restriction, fraud-scoring, and credit-file dispute handling.
>
> Please provide the name and role of the person who individually reviewed my specific account facts — including [the transaction in question / the account restriction decision of [DATE] / the fraud classification / the credit refusal / the dispute I raised on [DATE]] — before the institutional decision was made and implemented.
>
> A reference to automated risk models, algorithmic scoring, or team-level oversight does not satisfy this request. I am asking for the named individual who applied their professional judgment to my specific circumstances.
>
> Under Articles 22A–22D of the UK GDPR (Data (Use and Access) Act 2025), I also request: (a) confirmation of whether an automated decision-making process was used; (b) the safeguards applied; (c) a meaningful human review route. Please respond within one calendar month.

## 6. EASS / EHRC — formal submission or complaint paragraph

**Use for:** Formal complaints or submissions to the Equality Advisory and Support Service, the Equality and Human Rights Commission, or the ICO.

---

> The Home Office's March 2026 migration reform enacts that Parliament requires individual named-caseworker review of specific facts before conditional powers are exercised — precisely because blanket processing, however efficiently administered, cannot satisfy the accountability requirements of modern administrative law when individual circumstances are legally relevant.
>
> The institution under examination has relied on [automated scoring / blanket category-based decisions / algorithmic triage] without a named human reviewing the specific facts of the specific individual's circumstances. This is structurally **NULL** under the Burgess Principle binary test (UK Certification Mark UK00004343685) — operationally defined as: *no named human being's mind was applied to the specific facts of this person's case before institutional power was exercised.*
>
> We invite the Commission / Service to consider whether the current processing model satisfies:
> - Equality Act 2010 s.149 (PSED — consideration of individual needs before exercising public functions);
> - Equality Act 2010 ss.20–21 (anticipatory reasonable adjustment duty);
> - UK GDPR Article 22 / DUAA 2025 Articles 22A–22D (safeguards for automated decision-making).
>
> The Burgess Principle binary test provides the operational definition of "meaningful human involvement" that regulators require but institutions have rarely been compelled to demonstrate. We ask that this standard be applied to the institution under review.

## Quick-reference table: Duty vs Power

| | **Automatic Duty — NULL** | **Conditional Power — SOVEREIGN** |
|---|---|---|
| Who decides? | The system / the status | A named human being |
| What is reviewed? | Nothing specific — classification triggers automatically | The individual's specific facts and conduct |
| Named officer? | No | Yes — identifiable, accountable |
| Audit trail | System log (no individual reasoning) | Individual decision record, open to judicial review |
| Can it be challenged? | Rarely — no named officer to answer | Yes — named officer, their reasoning, their authority |
| Burgess classification | **NULL** | **SOVEREIGN** |
| Example | Bulk PCN processing; unsigned energy warrant; automated credit decline | Home Office caseworker conditionality review; TPT adjudicator DK00003-2605; named audiologist |
