## What This Register Is

The Burgess Principle exists to identify where human review is absent. This register documents the other side: practitioners and businesses who have been assessed and confirmed as meeting the SOVEREIGN standard in their day-to-day work.

A practitioner is **SOVEREIGN** when a named human being individually reviews the specific facts of every client case before any action is taken. No batch processing. No auto-generated outputs submitted without personal examination. One person. One case. Every time.

This is not a low bar dressed up as a high one. It is simply the standard that used to be assumed — and increasingly is not.
