# Getting Started — The Burgess Principle

**Five minutes to understand. Template letters ready to use.**

## If You Need To Act Today

1. Pick the closest scenario below.
2. Copy the matching letter.
3. Replace names, dates, and references.
4. Send it by email or post and keep proof of sending.
5. Classify the reply: **SOVEREIGN**, **NULL**, or **AMBIGUOUS**.

If the reply is **NULL** or **AMBIGUOUS**, move to escalation in the section called "What Happens Next".

If you are stressed and holding debt or enforcement letters right now, start
with [START_HERE_DEBT_LETTERS.md](./START_HERE_DEBT_LETTERS.md) for a one-page
starter kit and ready paste blocks for AI/chatbots.

## The 5-Minute Overview

An AI system denied you a service.  
An energy company forced entry to your home without proper review.  
A benefits department rejected your claim with a form letter.  
Your content was removed by algorithmic moderation.

In each case, you can ask one question:

> **"Was a human member of the team able to personally review the specific facts of my specific situation?"**

That human doesn't have to approve you. They just have to look at *you*—your facts, your context, your circumstances—*before* the decision was made.

If yes: **SOVEREIGN**. The system worked as it should.  
If no: **NULL**. The decision was automated bulk-processing, not human judgment.  
If they won't say clearly: **AMBIGUOUS**. They're hiding it, so you escalate.

## Evidence Checklist Before You Send

Collect and store these items in one folder:

- Decision letter, email, screenshot, or account notice
- Your account or claim reference number
- Dates and times of key events
- Any disability or adjustment requests you already made
- A timeline of what happened in 5 to 10 bullet points

This makes follow-up, ombudsman, or regulator escalation much faster.

### 1. Energy / Utilities Dispute

**Use this if:** Your energy company processed a decision (meter reading, forced entry, billing adjustment) and cannot name who reviewed your specific case.

Typical evidence to attach: bill history, warrant notice, meter photos, previous complaint references.

```text
[Your name]
[Your address]
[Date]

[Company Name]  
[Company Address]

Re: Request for confirmation of individual human review — [Account ref / situation]

Dear Sir / Madam,

On [date], your organisation made the following decision affecting me:
[describe the decision: billing adjustment, warrant, refusal to amend bill, etc.]

I am writing to ask for confirmation that a named human member of your team 
personally reviewed the specific facts of my specific situation before this 
decision was made.

Please provide:

1. The name and role of the human who reviewed my case
2. A brief note of the specific facts about me/my account that they reviewed
3. The date on which that review took place
4. Confirmation that this review occurred before the decision was made

If no such review took place, please say so clearly. If the decision was 
made by automated process without individual human review, that is also 
an accurate answer.

I am asking this question under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, 
which requires "meaningful human involvement" in automated decisions 
affecting individuals.

Please reply within 14 days.

Yours faithfully,
[Your signature]
[Your name]
```

### 2. Benefits / Social Security Claim

**Use this if:** A benefits authority rejected or reduced your claim via an automated letter or form without explaining who reviewed your individual circumstances.

Typical evidence to attach: decision letter, assessment notes, adjustment requests, medical or caring evidence already submitted.

```text
[Your name]
[Your address]
[Date]

[Department / Local Authority]
[Address]

Re: Request for confirmation of individual human review — [Claim ref / date of decision]

Dear Sir / Madam,

On [date], your department issued a decision on my [benefit type] claim:
[describe: rejection, reduction, suspension, etc.]

I am writing to ask for confirmation that a named human member of your team 
personally reviewed the specific facts of my specific situation before 
this decision was made.

Please provide:

1. The name and role of the person who made the final decision in my case
2. What specific facts about me (including any circumstances, declarations, 
   or personal health/disability/caring factors I provided) they reviewed
3. The date on which that review took place
4. Confirmation that this review happened before the decision was issued

If my case was determined by automated system without individual review, 
please state that clearly.

I am entitled to information about individual human involvement in this 
decision under UK GDPR Article 22 and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.

Please reply within 20 working days.

Yours faithfully,
[Your signature]
[Your name]
```

### 3. Lending / Credit Decision

**Use this if:** A lender, credit provider, or fintech company rejected your application with a web form response and did not explain who reviewed your specific circumstances.

Typical evidence to attach: application reference, rejection message, any affordability context the form did not capture.

**Mortgage or insurance instead?** Use the dedicated copy-paste letters:

- Mortgage refusal, product transfer, remortgage, or forbearance → [`templates/MORTGAGE_DECISION_WITH_BURGESS.md`](./templates/MORTGAGE_DECISION_WITH_BURGESS.md)
- Insurance claim, quote refusal, non-renewal, or automated underwriting → [`templates/INSURANCE_DECISION_WITH_BURGESS.md`](./templates/INSURANCE_DECISION_WITH_BURGESS.md)

```text
[Your name]
[Your address]
[Date]

[Lender / Credit Company]
[Address]

Re: Request for confirmation of individual human review — [Application ref]

Dear Sir / Madam,

I applied to you for [loan / credit / mortgage / etc.] on [date].
On [date], I received a decision of rejection / suspension / referral to manual review.

I am writing to ask for confirmation that a named human member of your team 
personally reviewed the specific facts of my application before this decision was made.

Please provide:

1. The name and role of the human decision-maker
2. What specific financial or personal facts in my application they reviewed
3. The date on which the review took place
4. Confirmation that this review occurred before the decision was sent to me

If my application was scored and rejected by automated credit assessment 
without individual human review, please confirm that.

This information is required under UK GDPR Article 22 (right to explanation 
in automated decision-making) and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 
(meaningful human involvement in automated decisions).

Please reply within 14 days.

Yours faithfully,
[Your signature]
[Your name]
```

### 4. Platform Content Moderation

**Use this if:** A social media, streaming, or content platform removed your content, suspended your account, or restricted your access via an automated system without human review of your specific context.

Typical evidence to attach: content ID, enforcement notice, appeal history, account-history context.

```text
[Your name / account handle]
[Your email or account reference]
[Date]

[Platform name] Trust & Safety Team
[Support contact / legal address]

Re: Request for confirmation of individual human review — [Content ID / Account suspension ref]

Dear Sir / Madam,

On [date], your platform took the following action on my account / content:
[describe: removal, suspension, demonetisation, etc.]

Your notification stated: [quote their reason if available, or describe their response]

I am writing to request confirmation that a named human member of your team 
personally reviewed the specific facts of my specific content / account / 
context before this enforcement action was taken.

In particular, I am asking whether a human reviewer considered:

[List specific context they should have reviewed: e.g., 
- The fact that my content is satire / commentary / educational
- My account history and previous compliance record
- My location and applicable local laws
- Any disability-related or accessibility accommodations I have requested
]

Please provide:

1. The name (or unique identifier) of the human reviewer who made this decision
2. What specific facts about my content or account they reviewed
3. The date of that review
4. Confirmation it occurred before the enforcement action

If this action was taken by automated system without individual human review, 
please state that clearly.

Under the Online Safety Bill / Digital Services Act / and emerging platform 
accountability standards, I am entitled to clarity on meaningful human 
involvement in this decision.

Please reply within 14 days.

Yours faithfully,
[Your signature]
[Your name]
```

### 5. Reasonable Adjustments / Disability Discrimination

**Use this if:** You have a disability or communication need, asked for reasonable adjustments, and the organisation refused or failed to provide them without explaining whether a human reviewed your specific circumstances and disability needs.

Typical evidence to attach: adjustment request, diagnosis or support evidence you are comfortable sharing, records of inaccessible communication.

```text
[Your name]
[Your address]
[Date]

[Organisation name]
[Address]

Re: Urgent – Request for confirmation of individual human review and 
reasonable adjustments — [Reference number / date of incident]

Dear Sir / Madam,

I am writing to request confirmation of individual human review in relation to 
the following:

[Describe the situation: e.g., 
- I requested [specific reasonable adjustment: large-print letters, telephone contact, 
  BSL interpreter, simplified language, extra time, etc.]
- On [date], your organisation refused / failed to provide this adjustment
- The response was: [quote their response or describe it]
]

I have a registered disability / communication need [specify if you are comfortable 
doing so: autism, hearing loss, dyslexia, chronic pain, etc.].

I am writing to ask: did a named human member of your team personally review 
my specific disability, my specific request for adjustment, and my specific 
circumstances before making this decision?

Please provide:

1. The name and role of the person who made the decision about my adjustment request
2. What specific facts about my disability and my needs they reviewed
3. Evidence that they considered the Equality Act 2010 s.20 (duty to make 
   reasonable adjustments) and my specific situation
4. The date on which this review took place
5. Their specific reasoning for the decision made

If no such human review took place, please state that clearly.

I am entitled to this information under:
- Equality Act 2010 ss.19–21 (protection from discrimination)
- Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (meaningful human involvement)
- UK GDPR Article 22

Please reply within 10 working days. Failure to provide reasonable adjustments 
and to review my individual circumstances may constitute discrimination.

Yours faithfully,
[Your signature]
[Your name]
```

## What Happens Next

### If They Answer with a Name and Facts (→ SOVEREIGN)

Congratulations. You have clarity. A human did review your case. Document their response and keep it safe.

If the decision is still wrong in law, you can now challenge it on the merits—not on the grounds that it was automated without review.

---

### If They Avoid the Question / Use Vague Language (→ AMBIGUOUS)

Example responses:

- "We have a human review layer in our process."
- "Our system is subject to human oversight."
- "All decisions are reviewed for compliance."
- "We take individual circumstances into account in our algorithm."

**None of these answer the question.** They are evasion. Send a follow-up:

Best practice: quote their exact wording, ask for a direct YES or NO, and give a clear reply deadline.

```text
[Your name]
[Date]

[Organisation]

Re: Follow-up – Clarification required on individual human review

Dear Sir / Madam,

I received your letter of [date] in response to my request for confirmation 
of individual human review.

You stated: [quote their evasive language]

This does not answer my question. I am asking specifically:

- Can you name the individual human being who reviewed my case?
- Can you describe what specific facts about me they reviewed?
- Can you confirm that review occurred before the decision was made?

Please reply with a clear YES or NO to each question, or provide the specific 
name and facts if the answer is YES.

If the answer is NO, please state that clearly.

Yours faithfully,
[Your signature]
[Your name]
```

---

### If They Say No Individual Review Happened (→ NULL)

**This is the evidence you need.** They have admitted that your decision was automated bulk-processing without individual review.

Under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, Articles 22A–22D, this admission means:

1. The decision may be unlawful (lack of meaningful human involvement)
2. You are entitled to escalation to a human decision-maker
3. You may have grounds for a formal complaint to the data protection regulator or ombudsman
4. If the decision harmed you financially or otherwise, you may have grounds for compensation

**Next steps:**

- **Energy/utilities:** File a complaint with the [Energy Ombudsman](https://www.energyombudsman.org.uk/)
- **Public benefits:** File with the [Local Government Ombudsman](https://www.lgo.org.uk/) (if local authority) or [Parliamentary Ombudsman](https://www.ombudsman.org.uk/) (if central government)
- **Lending/credit, mortgage, or insurance:** File with the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/)
- **All organisations:** Submit a complaint to the [ICO](https://www.ico.org.uk/) under GDPR Article 22

Keep a simple evidence log with: date sent, date replied, who replied, classification result, and escalation submitted.
