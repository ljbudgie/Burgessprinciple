# Accessibility — Start Here If You Have Access Needs

This page is written in plain language. Short sentences. One idea at a time.

The Burgess Principle was built by a deaf man because nobody was looking at
his specific situation. Access needs are not an afterthought here. They are the
reason the project exists.

You do not need to read the whole repository. Pick the section below that fits
you and ignore the rest.

---

## The one question

> **"Was a human member of the team able to personally review the specific
> facts of my specific situation?"**

- Yes, and they can name the person — **SOVEREIGN**.
- No human looked at your case — **NULL**.
- A vague answer ("human oversight", "in line with policy") — **AMBIGUOUS**,
  which is not a yes.

That is the whole test. Everything else in this repository helps you ask it,
record the answer, and escalate when the answer is not a real yes.

## Your rights in one paragraph

If you are disabled or have a communication need, the Equality Act 2010
(sections 20 and 29) gives you the right to **reasonable adjustments**. An
institution must adjust how it communicates with you and how it makes decisions
about you. Asking for an adjustment in writing is normal, free, and does not
require a lawyer. Every UK letter template in this repository already carries a
reasonable-adjustment footer you can adapt.

## If you are deaf or hard of hearing

- **Everything here works by text.** No phone calls are ever required. Every
  template is a written letter or email.
- Use the **[BSL adaptations template](./templates/BSL_ADAPTATIONS.md)** if
  British Sign Language is your first or preferred language. It requests BSL
  interpreters or video messages, with email or text as a backup.
- Ask for **email-only contact** as a reasonable adjustment. If an institution
  directs you to a telephone number after you told them you are email-only,
  that is itself evidence — record it.
- **[OpenHear](https://github.com/ljbudgie/openhear)** is the ecosystem's
  sovereign audio pipeline for hearing aid users. It keeps processed sound
  under your control where assistive technology mediates communication.

## If you are blind or use a screen reader

- Every document in this repository is **plain Markdown**: real headings, real
  lists, real link text. It reads cleanly in any screen reader, in your
  terminal, or in the GitHub web interface.
- **[Iris](./iris.html)** works in the browser with typed input — no account,
  no install. You can also paste any template into the AI assistant you
  already use with your own accessibility setup.
- Templates are copy-paste text. Nothing depends on images, colour, or layout.

## If reading or processing dense text is hard

(Cognitive disability, learning disability, neurodivergence, brain injury,
fatigue, or simply overload.)

- Start with **[START_HERE.md](./START_HERE.md)** — one question, one page.
- Then **[CHOOSE_YOUR_PATH.md](./CHOOSE_YOUR_PATH.md)** — pick one path,
  ignore the rest.
- The **[common scenarios guide](./templates/COMMON_SCENARIOS.md)** routes you
  straight to the right letter. You fill in the brackets. You do not have to
  write anything from scratch.
- You can ask for **extra time** and **simplified language** as reasonable
  adjustments. The [Equality Act template](./templates/EQUALITY_ACT_WITH_BURGESS_PRINCIPLE.md)
  covers this.

## If speaking, phone calls, or face-to-face contact are barriers

(Non-verbal communication, AAC use, anxiety, autism, or situational barriers.)

- The entire framework is designed to work **in writing**. A calm letter is the
  primary tool, not a fallback.
- **[Advocate Companion](https://github.com/ljbudgie/advocate-companion)** is
  the ecosystem's practical advocacy interface — a privacy-first app that
  helps you turn what happened into a clear, calm adjustment request or
  escalation message, and keeps your records local.

## If energy, pain, or executive function are limited

(Chronic illness, energy-limiting conditions, mental health, hidden
disabilities.)

- One letter is enough to start. Send
  [`REQUEST_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW.md`](./templates/REQUEST_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW.md)
  and stop. The follow-up templates exist for later, when you have capacity.
- Keep copies. The record does the work so you do not have to hold it in your
  head.
- If a benefits decision is involved, use
  [`BENEFITS_CLAIM_HELP.md`](./templates/BENEFITS_CLAIM_HELP.md).

## If this page does not cover your access need

Open an issue on this repository describing the barrier, or adapt
[`ACCESSIBILITY_REASONABLE_ADJUSTMENTS_WITH_BURGESS.md`](./templates/ACCESSIBILITY_REASONABLE_ADJUSTMENTS_WITH_BURGESS.md)
— it is deliberately written to fit any adjustment, not a fixed list. Nothing
in this framework assumes you can hear, speak, see a screen, read quickly, or
have energy to spare. If something here does assume that, it is a bug —
please tell us.
