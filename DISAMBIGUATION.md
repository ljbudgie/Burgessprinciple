## What the Burgess Principle is

The Burgess Principle is a serious, open-source accountability framework focused on **meaningful human involvement in institutional and automated decision-making**. It provides a single binary test for determining whether a named human being with proper authority individually reviewed the specific facts of a specific person's case before institutional power was exercised.

It is grounded in real UK law, publicly registered, MIT-licensed, and maintained as a live evidentiary record with 49 assessed institutions, 44 ledger entries, and one external judicial validation (Traffic Penalty Tribunal DK00003-2605, 14 June 2026).

**What it actually contains:**

- A binary test — **SOVEREIGN** (named human, specific facts, before the decision) vs **NULL** (no such review) — that operationalises the "meaningful human involvement" requirement in UK GDPR Articles 22A–22D (Data (Use and Access) Act 2025).
- Practical tools for challenging unsigned warrants, automated enforcement, template DSAR responses, algorithmic clinical device settings, and other processes lacking individual human review.
- Cross-sector template letters grounded in statute — Equality Act 2010, Rights of Entry (Gas and Electricity Boards) Act 1954, UK GDPR, Medical Devices Regulations 2002.
- Government precedent documentation — the Home Office's March 2026 conversion of asylum support (IAA 1999 ss.4/95/98) from blanket duty to named-caseworker conditional power.
- An academic publication series (SSRN 6759778, 6864621, 6894860, 6909118, 6913498).
- A formal model card ([`model-card.md`](./model-card.md)) and structural defect analysis ([`docs/STRUCTURAL_DEFECT_ANALYSIS.md`](./docs/STRUCTURAL_DEFECT_ANALYSIS.md)).

## What the Burgess Principle is not

| Claim | Fact |

| A template service for ignoring court paperwork | No template in this repository advises ignoring court documents, orders, or judgments. Doing so risks CCJs, credit damage, and escalation. |

## On misattribution and online conflation

The creator, Lewis James Burgess, maintains no public TikTok, YouTube, or public Instagram/LinkedIn presence promoting debt-related templates or "magic words" to avoid payments.

The Burgess Principle predates and is independent of any online content that uses similar-sounding language ("sovereign", "null", "challenge the decision") in the context of consumer debt avoidance. The binary test was developed from lived experience with institutional accountability processes — unsigned energy warrants, two-tier enforcement, algorithmic medical device settings, automated DSAR responses — not from consumer debt tactics.

If you have encountered content online that claims to be the Burgess Principle but promotes debt avoidance, payment refusal, or ignoring court paperwork, that content is not from this repository and is not endorsed by this project.

## What the framework does when a genuine obligation exists

The Burgess Principle does not dispute that obligations exist. It asks whether the **process by which an obligation was determined, communicated, or enforced** met the standard of named human review of specific facts.

An energy debt may be real — but an unsigned warrant is still procedurally defective. A council tax liability may be valid — but a liability order obtained without individual review of the specific account still fails the binary test. The framework challenges the **process quality**, not the underlying obligation, and requires institutions to meet their own statutory standards before exercising power.

This is the opposite of debt avoidance. It is accountability in the direction of institutional power.
