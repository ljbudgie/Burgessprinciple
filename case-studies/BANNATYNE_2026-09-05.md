He later pasted today's clause 7 from that URL. He did not attach the 9 January 2025 welcome email, the terms version current on that date, or the tick audit.

6. Duncan Bannatyne OBE (CEO & Chairman) wrote the same evening. He objected to tone, said he would elect "one person only" to rectify any mistakes and to take action on unpaid debt "in accordance with your contract," asked that nobody else at the company be contacted, and said it was not in employees' health and wellness interest to deal with the member. He did not name the elected person. He did not attach the 2025 file.
