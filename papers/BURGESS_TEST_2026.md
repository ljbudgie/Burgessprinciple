## Abstract summary

The paper operationalises the statutory requirement for meaningful human
involvement in automated decisions. It documents 18 institutional audits,
11 confirmed NULL findings, and the full Burgess Principle binary test
methodology. It identifies a regulatory compliance gap across three concurrent
frameworks — the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, the EU AI Act, and the NIST AI
Risk Management Framework — and proposes the Burgess Test as the only published
operational definition filling that gap.
