## The constraint that governs every name

The device proves **non-repudiable named accountability** — "a specific human put
their name to this and can be held to it" — **not** that the human genuinely
reviewed (the cognition gap; see the concept note §2). So the name must evoke *a
person binding their name to a decision*, and must never imply mind-reading or
legal-grade proof. This rules out the whole "proof of thought" family
(*ProofMind*, *TrueReview*, *Verify*-anything).

## The existing naming houses (stay coherent with these)

- **Warm, single-word, human:** *Iris* — the eye that sees; calm; a messenger.
- **Functional / "Sovereign-" prefixed:** *OpenHear, Sovereign Vault, Sovereign Core.*
- **Founder-anchored standard:** *Burgess Principle / Burgess Test.*

## Proposed lexicon — a layered family, not one label

| Layer | Proposed name | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Programme / family** | **Burgess Witness** | "Witness" carries legal weight (witness statement, *bearing* witness); plain, dignified; pairs with the evidence/ledger framing. The umbrella. |
| **The device** | **Signet** | A signet ring is literally the original personal-seal device — a named individual pressing their own mark to take responsibility. A modern wristband/fob "Signet" is precise, tactile, and instantly understood. |
| **The act / credential** | **Seal** | Gives a verb (*seal it*), a noun (*a sealed attestation*), and is the most exact word for personal non-repudiable authority. Quietly scriptural (signet/seal imagery in the restoration narrative). |
| **Trust root** | **Burgess Attestor Registry** | Already named; "attestor" lives at the protocol/registry layer. |
| **Companion AI** | **Iris** | Already named; prompts the question and surfaces the facts. |

### How the stack reads

> **Iris** prompts the question → a named human applies their **Signet** →
> producing a **Seal** → verifiable against the **Burgess Attestor Registry** →
> all under the **Burgess Witness** programme.

Eye, hand, mark, registry. There is also a deliberate *Iris (the eye that sees) /
Signet (the hand that signs)* duality.

## Alternatives, honestly weighed

- **Keeper** — from the *Restored Keeper* paper (*"am I my brother's keeper?"*).
  Doctrinally beautiful; held in reserve as the poetic alternative to *Signet* if
  that hits a trademark wall, or if the philosophical name is preferred over the
  functional one.
- **Sovereign Attestor** (the original working name) — accurate but cold and
  technical; retired to the *registry/protocol* layer rather than the consumer
  device.

If a flag must be planted: **Burgess Witness (family) · Signet (device) · Seal
(act)**, with **Keeper** in reserve.

## Honest pitfalls (resolve before any public use)

- **Trademark clearance required.** "Witness," "Seal," and especially **"Signet"**
  are common; *Signet Jewelers* is a large registered mark, so classes and
  clearances matter. Protection here rests primarily on the **certification mark
  (UK00004343685) on the standard**, not on a product wordmark — but clear names
  before printing anything.
- **"Seal" is a metaphor, not a legal seal.** It must not imply an eIDAS qualified
  electronic signature or a statutory seal (same boundary as the registry design
  §11). Brand language, not legal claim.
- **"Witness" is heavily used** as an app/product word, so the family name leans on
  "Burgess" for distinctiveness — which is fine; it is your standard.
