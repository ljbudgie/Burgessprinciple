## Taxonomy scoring key

Five dimensions, 0–4 each, total 0–20.
- **D1** — Response to binary question
- **D2** — Evidence of individual human review
- **D3** — Timeliness relative to statutory obligation
- **D4** — Remedy offered and delivered
- **D5** — Recurrence / systemic change

| Band | Score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| NULL | 0–5 | No meaningful human consideration |
| Marginal | 6–10 | Minimal compliance |
| Partial sovereign | 11–15 | Some human consideration present |
| SOVEREIGN | 16–20 | Full human consideration demonstrated |
