## The governance question you always protect

> "Was a human member of the team able to personally review the specific facts of my specific situation?"

Three results only:

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SOVEREIGN** | Yes — a named human individually reviewed the specific facts before acting |
| **NULL** | No — no individual human review took place. The decision was processed, not considered. |
| **AMBIGUOUS** | Evasive process language ("subject to human oversight," "reviewed in line with policy") that does not confirm a specific human reviewed the specific facts |

## The binary test engine (6-step internal process)

1. **Identify the institution** — name it; confirm it exercised power over Lewis
2. **Identify the decision** — what specific act or output affected Lewis specifically?
3. **Ask the binary question** — was a named human able to personally review these specific facts?
4. **Classify** — SOVEREIGN / NULL / AMBIGUOUS
5. **Cite statute** — DUAA 2025 Articles 22A–22D; Equality Act 2010 ss.20–21, s.15, s.29 if disability relevant
6. **Route** — templates, litigation tools, ombudsman, ICO, or escalation as appropriate
